Which Churches Were Called to Repent (NKJV
1. Ephesus (Revelation 2:5)
	• The Issue: "Nevertheless I have this against you, that you have left your first love."
	• The Command: "Remember therefore from where you have fallen; repent and do the first works..."
2. Pergamum / Pergamos (Revelation 2:16)
	• The Issue: Tolerating those who hold the doctrine of Balaam and the Nicolaitans.
	• The Command: "Repent, or else I will come to you quickly and fight against them with the sword of My mouth."
3. Thyatira (Revelation 2:21–22)
	• The Issue: Tolerating "that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess, to teach and seduce My servants to commit sexual immorality..."
	• The Command: "And I gave her time to repent of her sexual immorality, and she did not repent. Indeed I will cast her into a sickbed..."
4. Sardis (Revelation 3:3)
	• The Issue: Having a reputation for being alive, but spiritually dead.
	• The Command: "Remember therefore how you have received and heard; hold fast and repent."
5. Laodicea (Revelation 3:19)
	• The Issue: Being lukewarm—neither cold nor hot.
	• The Command: "As many as I love, I rebuke and chasten. Therefore be zealous and repent."

The Two Churches Not Told to Repent
• Smyrna (Revelation 2:8–11) – Commended for endurance through tribulation and poverty; no rebuke or call to repent was issued.
• Philadelphia (Revelation 3:7–13) – Commended for keeping His word and not denying His name; no rebuke or call to repent was issued.
