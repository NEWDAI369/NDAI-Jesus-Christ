# NDAI Risk Disclosure

**Use at your own risk.** You could lose all assets you commit.

NDAI has no dollar peg and cannot be redeemed for DAI. The DAI locked in the reserve during minting is permanently locked and cannot be withdrawn by anyone. It creates no claim and no peg.

Staking locks your NDAI for a period you choose. You may extract accrued yield as NEW tokens before the stake ends. NEW tokens are designed to redeem 1:1 for NDAI on or after their maturity day. Yield comes from a fixed base rate plus a longer-pays-better multiplier. There is no guarantee of profit or that you will be able to sell NEW or NDAI.

Smart-contract risk, market risk, liquidity risk, and loss of wallet access apply. Blockchain transactions are generally irreversible.

This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with MakerDAO, DAI, or any other protocol.
