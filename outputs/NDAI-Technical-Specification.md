# NDAI technical specification

**Accounting amendment.** The [accounting amendment](NDAI-Accounting-Amendment.md) defines confirmed same-series retirement, candidate reward-index and activation mechanics, and acceptance test vectors. Existing fee-vault decisions remain in force pending explicit approval of any changes to activation, weighting or custody.

**Status:** Design for approval, 2026-11-14. This specification supersedes the prototype's economics where they differ. Proposed parameters require explicit approval. Existing fork results describe the prototype, whose unrestricted maturity days and fee-free claim issuance differ from this brief.

## 1. Confirmed requirements

- Production contracts are PulseChain-only. Each independently deployable NDAI token, staking contract, fee vault, claim series, and custody lock must reject deployment unless the EVM chain ID is 369. The official deployment and wallet interface also verify the network and approved contract addresses. A private fork can report 369, so chain ID alone does not authenticate mainnet.

- Independent PulseChain protocol. Earlier NDAI contracts are historical reference. Staking uses NDAI directly. Actuator informs the design of transferable, dated claims and optional issuance. There is no HEX, Hedron, Cities staking, seven-day qualification, day 40â€“47 exit window, T-share formula, ACTR emission, native yield or initial amplification.
- Mint payment is pDAI via PulseX V1, in the sequence pDAI â†’ PLSX â†’ WPLS â†’ bridged DAI. Each successful mint credits at least one newly received DAI per NDAI in whole-token equivalents to a permanent, nonredeemable reserve. Each mint is atomic. NDAI carries no DAI claim, USD peg, oracle pricing or promised market value.
- One standard staking maturity option at launch. Ordinary stake and matured withdrawal each cost 0% in protocol fees. Early exits are limited to unencumbered principal, under a policy awaiting approval.
- Optional transferable time claims are fully funded in NDAI. At maturity, a holder burns a claim for one NDAI per whole claim. Position ownership and claim ownership are independent. Transferring a position preserves its obligations.
- Issuance charges 1% of gross claim units from within that gross amount when the matching fee vault has eligible deposits. With zero eligible deposits, the fee is waived. Fee rewards come from claims actually collected. Distributing a fee claim transfers an existing claim, and creates neither new NDAI nor new claims. Zero collected fees means zero new rewards.
- Independent audit and remediation are mandatory before real funds. The December 25, 2026 08:00 UTC cutoff and immutability were earlier requirements. Confirm the schedule against audit completion and confirm the permission model in D16.

## 2. Pre-coding decision register

**Fee-vault accounting approved.** The following decisions apply to D06, D07, D09, D10 and D13. The AMA remains the architectural reference for same-date fee sharing and funded claims. The withdrawal order and rounding below are NDAI-specific rules.

- Partial withdrawals consume pending tranches newest first, then eligible balances. Preserve fee claims already earned.
- Fee payers share pro rata when their deposit was already eligible in the matching vault at issuance.
- Recipients may claim and transfer allocated time tokens immediately. Redemption for NDAI starts at the series redemption date.
- When the issuance fee applies, round it up to the nearest base unit. Round early-withdrawal burns down to the nearest base unit. Keep reward rounding dust reserved for the same series.

**Fee-vault selection approved.** Users choose a fee vault by its associated time-token redemption date from the enabled series. They deposit NDAI directly into that vault. Each account has a separate 90-day withdrawal clock per vault, measured from its latest deposit or addition in that vault. The redemption date determines which issuance fees the vault receives and when the fee claims can be redeemed for NDAI. The 90-day clock determines the withdrawal penalty on the user's deposited NDAI, including when that clock extends beyond the redemption date. At launch, the selection list contains one enabled redemption date. The first date and future listing policy remain open.

**Minting decision approved.** Keep the original pDAI payment and PulseX V1 reserve route. Budget an additional **20% of the quoted pDAI reserve swap cost** to add pDAI/PLSX liquidity during each mint. Mint the resulting LP tokens directly to the permanent, non-withdrawable lock. The reserve purchase must still deliver at least one newly received bridged DAI per NDAI minted. This confirms the mint allocation and its purpose. The Actuator-inspired staking and claim design remains subject to the decisions below.

## 4. State machines

**Position:** Absent â†’ Active after successful full NDAI receipt. Active positions hold both free and encumbered balances. Issue only within the confirmed issuance window and free balance. Retirement moves encumbered principal back to free principal. Early exit reduces only the free balance and settles eligibility before the balance change. Transfer updates ownership atomically, including rights attached to the position, without moving or releasing collateral. At maturity the position can withdraw its free balance once, paying no ordinary fee. Closing the NFT leaves independently funded series claims intact. Keep reward rights claimable after principal withdrawal through a retained record or an atomic checkpoint to a wallet entitlement. No transition iterates over all positions.

**Claim:** Before maturity, issue gross units after free principal is moved to series collateral. Credit net to minter and fee to escrow in the same transaction. Transfer leaves debt unchanged. Before maturity, retirement is authorized by the position owner and claim holder (same account by default). Burn same-series claims and decrease the designated position's assigned debt by that amount, then return equal collateral to its free balance. A matured holder can redeem without identifying the originating position. Consume units, write reductions, then transfer corresponding NDAI in the same atomic transaction. A failed transfer reverts consumption and accounting.

**Fungibility and compartment accounting:** Before maturity, maintain per-position attributed debt and per-series aggregate outstanding supply. Two funding buckets track user and fee claim collateral. They represent funding origin, since transferable tokens of a series are fungible. For retirement or redemption, consume backing with a deterministic debit order, proposed user bucket first and fee bucket second. A debit always reduces the aggregate liability and pays/releases exactly one unit of backing per unit burned. Retirement additionally reduces only the retiring position's debt. Keep both bucket totals nonnegative and sum equal to outstanding supply. At maturity, disable issuance and retirement. Position debt at cutoff remains the amount excluded from the owner's principal payout, even if a holder has since redeemed against the series pool. Never add position debt to the series liability a second time. A separate fee claim inventory ledger follows token ownership and entitlements. If approval instead requires fee backing to follow each individual token, fungibility would require a revised token model with separate classes. Approve this distinction as part of D07/D13.

**Fee vault and rewards.** Track each accountâ€™s deposited NDAI, latest addition time, pending activation tranches, eligible balance, and accrued fee claims separately for each redemption date. A positive deposit resets the whole account balanceâ€™s withdrawal timer in that vault. Only new units wait 24 hours. At issuance, include activations due at or before that timestamp and allocate using the resulting eligible balances. A series-local reward index with pre-change checkpoints is proposed. Keep earned entitlements after withdrawal. Exact checkpoint precision and activation batching remain open.

### Weight comparison

| Model | Distribution weight | Concentration, timing, gas and fairness |
|---|---|---|
| A | Eligible staked principal at finalized snapshot | Lowest accounting cost, proportional and easy to explain. Large balances dominate. A spot snapshot invites just-in-time stake capture unless eligibility predates the snapshot and stakes remain qualified. Smaller participants can lose out to large transient positions. |
| B | Eligible principal Ã— committed or remaining time | Committed duration is equivalent to A when all commitments match. Remaining duration changes weight over time and can favor early entrants. Fixed lock terms plus cooldown reduce flash capture. Duration multipliers magnify large long positions. Updating remaining duration raises accounting cost and cutoff sensitivity. |
| C | Integral of eligible balance over an epoch | Proportional to capital and time. Momentary capital earns zero or little weight, with seasoning further reducing capture. Large persistent stakes still dominate. Small stakes earn proportional dust, and claims can be batched to reduce gas. Requires checkpointing and bounded settlement. Each epoch can distribute the exact collected denomination. |

**Final reward-weight rule.** At each issuance, snapshot eligible NDAI principal in the matching fee vault after processing all tranches with activation timestamp at or before the fee timestamp. Each tranche activates at its deposit block timestamp plus exactly 86,400 seconds. Each eligible NDAI earns the same share per token within its matching vault, with no extra multiplier for deposit size or lock duration. For example, 200 eligible NDAI receives twice the allocation of 100 eligible NDAI before rounding. Rewards come only from issuance fees actually collected. Launch has one shared redemption date. If additional series are enabled, users can choose among the available redemption dates. Ordinary calls process activation automatically. For collected fee F, total eligible balance E and eligible balance B, allocate F times B divided by E before rounding. If E is zero, waive the fee and reserve the full gross issuance. The accounting amendment compares weights and specifies the provisional 10^27 index, bounded scheduling and test vectors. The AMA informs the fee-sharing concept. The timing rule is an NDAI project decision.

Example: 100 fee claims and eligible deposits of 1,000 and 3,000 NDAI allocate 25 and 75 claims. With no eligible deposits, there is no charge and no backlog. Reward-index precision and the exact handling of reserved residuals remain open.

## 6. Invariants and threat model

- **Supply and DAI.** For each successful mint, measured new reserve DAI meets normalized required output. Cumulative measured deposits bound cumulative reserve-backed issuance, subject to explicit decimal normalization. Total supply equals cumulative mint minus actual burns. No alternative mint authority. Failed mint changes no protocol balances, approvals or supply. Trace pDAI spend/refund separately from network gas.
- **Collateral conservation.** Before maturity, each position's free units plus attributed encumbrance equals remaining deposited principal. At and after maturity, user withdrawal may use only free units. For each series, actual free custody â‰¥ sum of free balances and actual reserved NDAI â‰¥ outstanding claim supply at normalized decimals. No vault counts the same NDAI in both. Fee inventory backs assigned rewards plus undistributed amounts, with each token counted once. Redemption uses NDAI regardless of DAI reserve or market liquidity.
- **Authorization and reentrancy.** An unrelated account cannot withdraw, issue against, retire against or redirect a position. Transfer and approval permissions must be precisely scoped. Validate token identities and use checked transfers, state changes before outbound calls, and guard all paths that can reenter through routers, tokens or receiver callbacks. Include atomic NFT acceptance with expected terms if approved. Preserve permissionless mature claims.
- **Market and execution.** Thin liquidity, donation-skewed pools, manipulated spot quotes, sandwiching and reorgs may cause worse prices or failed transactions. Use pinned simulation state, canonicality checks, bounded allowance, signed slippage and deadlines. A slippage bound does not validate fair market value. Reserve accounting rejects short receipt regardless of upstream quote. Validate dust, zero outputs, fee-on-transfer, rebasing and nonstandard input assumptions. External dependencies may change behavior or become unavailable.
- **Rewards and availability.** Test same-block deposit/issue/withdraw cycles, checkpoint boundaries, self-dealing through several positions, NFT transfer before/after seal, empty epochs, double claims, donations and one-wei fragmentation. Prevent unbounded loops and settle long gaps incrementally with liveness testing. Reconciliations use independently verified events and balances. No discretionary seizure for unclaimed proceeds.
- **Trust boundaries.** Users trust wallet signing, transaction inclusion and RPC observations. The reserve depends on the DAI token and bridge. Minting depends on the immutable address selection and ongoing route availability. Any proposed operational role must be documented field by field, with privilege denial tests. Require an independent audit, fixes and audit follow-up before real funds.
